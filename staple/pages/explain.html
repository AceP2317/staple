Purpose

Staple plans a repair fleet's next day. It decides who takes each call, what each truck carries, and what people do before {{day_start}}, so more repairs finish on the first visit.

Key points

  • Every call, technician, part and dollar is invented. The method is real, and nothing here shows the gain on a real fleet.
  • It is scored as a back-test. Each method learns from the earlier days, then plans the last {{test_days}} days unseen.
  • Over those days Staple beats the current rules on all {{measures}} measures in the scorecard.
  • Every gain sits beside a control, which is the method run again with one link in its data broken.
  • Claude codes every booking from the customer's own words. Staple plans the trucks, and people act before {{day_start}}.

Functions

The scorecard
The whole plan against the current rules, measure by measure.
To do before {{day_start}}
What the parts room, the call center, dispatch and the buyer each do.
The board
Every call on every truck for one invented day, as planned at night and as the day ran.
The route map
Choose a technician to see their route, their to-do items and their load sheet.
The evidence
{{cards}} cards, each opening with its finding, then its chart and its control.
Instruction manual
Every label, status and threshold.
Methods page
Every formula, prompt, query and check.
